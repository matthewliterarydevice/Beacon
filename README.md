# Beacon

Beacon is a mobile app designed to help bystanders respond quickly and effectively when someone may be experiencing an opioid overdose. The app aims to bridge the gap between an emergency situation and timely support, especially in the critical first few minutes when rapid action can make the difference between life and death.

## Why Beacon Matters

Opioid overdoses can become life-threatening within minutes. Without oxygen to the brain, permanent brain damage can occur in as little as 3 to 5 minutes. Beacon is built to help bystanders take the right steps quickly, even if they are untrained or unsure what to do.

## The Problem Beacon Addresses

Beacon focuses on two urgent scenarios in the Vancouver area:

- In Downtown Vancouver, an untrained bystander may encounter an overdose with no trained responder nearby.
- In the Downtown Eastside, trained responders are often present but may need to manage the entire response alone, which can lead to burnout and reduce the quality of care.

## How Beacon Works

Beacon offers two response modes:

- Nudge Mode: guides an untrained bystander through a short series of steps to begin an overdose response.
- Alert Mode: calls 911 and notifies nearby trained responders at the same time.

Beacon is intended to support emergency services, not replace them. It is designed as a supplement to existing response systems and emergency care.

## Mission

Beacon aims to create a faster, more coordinated response to opioid overdoses by combining simple guidance for bystanders with rapid alerts to trained responders.

## MVP Goals

This MVP is being developed as a demo-focused prototype to showcase the core experience of Beacon. The initial goal is to validate the app’s concept, user flow, and value in a real-world context.

## Target Users

Beacon is designed for:

- Bystanders who witness a possible overdose and need clear guidance
- Trained responders who want a faster, more supported way to coordinate response efforts
- Community organizations and nonprofits that can help distribute and support the app

## Partnerships and Impact

Beacon is being developed with the aim of partnering with existing organizations and nonprofits to build trust, expand reach, and create a stronger foundation for real-world adoption.

## Project Status

This repository currently represents the early MVP stage of the Beacon product, with a focus on developing the front-end experience and preparing a strong prototype for demonstration and future validation.

## Next Steps

The immediate focus is on building a strong front-end prototype and gathering insight into the next steps needed to turn Beacon into a real, sustainable product in the business and community context.
